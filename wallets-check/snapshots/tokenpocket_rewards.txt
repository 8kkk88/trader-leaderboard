# TP Card Commission Rewards
TokenPocket will offer commission rewards for early TP Card users and quality communities. When you or your referrals top up the TP Card (no minimum top-up limit), you will receive a commission based on a percentage of the top-up amount. This commission can be withdrawn directly.
###
1. Main Fee Details
#### 1.1 Top-Up Fee
* Current fee rate: A top-up fee of 0.5% - 1% will be charged based on the top-up amount.
* Fee discount rules: The more valid new users you invite, the greater the discount (15% - 60%).
#### 1.2 Invitation Commission Mechanism
* Commission rules:
1. After inviting someone to create a TP Card, the inviter will permanently earn rewards based on the user's top-up fees according to their VIP level.
2. VIP level is determined by account points. Inviting one user earns you 1000 points, while the invited user earns 5000 points.
3. Invited users must complete NFT account creation
4. Registration, and top-up at least 100 USDC (or equivalent) within 30 days for the invitation to be valid.
5. Users will remain permanently bound to the inviter, allowing continuous commission earning.
6. Commissions are Instant Settlement and stored under "Unclaimed Rewards." The minimum withdrawal amount is 10 USDC.
* Commission Rate:
The commission rate will vary based on the inviter's user level. The higher the level, the higher the commission rate.
#### 1.3 User Level Design
* User levels are determined by points earned through top-ups, invites, and other interactions.
* Higher levels enjoy lower fees and higher commission percentages.
Premium NFT: Users with Premium NFTs will receive an extra 0.1% fee discount and an extra 10% commission bonus (not yet launched).
#### 1.4 Commission Rules
* Conditions: The invited user must complete a top-up of at least 100 USDC (or equivalent).
* Effective invitations: To be considered valid, the invited user must complete NFT creation, KYC verification, and top-up within 30 days. If this time limit is exceeded, the invitation is considered invalid.
* Unlimited Mechanism: Validly invited users can maintain a permanent referral relationship. As long as the invited user makes a deposit, the inviter will receive commission rewards based on their user level.
#### 1.5 Point Expiry
* Point validity: 6 months (180 days). For every 1 USDC top-up, you earn 1 point.
* VIP level determination: After each calendar month, the system will reduce points proportionally and re-evaluate user levels.
* VIP Level Determination: At the end of each calendar month, the system will proportionally deduct the user's points and re-evaluate their VIP level based on the remaining points.
###
2. User Operation Process
#### 2.1 Invitation Process
* Generate a personal invite code/link from your account’s Invite Friends feature.
* The new user registers using your invite code and completes identity verification.
* After the new user top-ups at least 100 USDC, you will earn rewards based on the commission rate.
#### 2.2 Level Upgrade Process
* Users can check the number of effective invited users via the app.
* The system will automatically upgrade the user's level based on the number of users they invite. Higher levels will enjoy better fee discounts and increased commission rates.
#### 2.3 Commission Withdrawal Process
* Users can check their commission earnings in real-time and withdraw them anytime.
* There is no limit on commission withdrawals, which will be sent in USDC to the wallet address linked to your card NFT.
* A signature verification from the linked wallet address is required to withdraw the commission.
* The minimum reward that can be claimed is 10 USDC.
