Phantom Referral Program guide
The Phantom Referral Program lets you invite friends to Phantom and earn 25% of the net trading fees Phantom receives from their eligible trades for three months. Your friend gets 10% of their own eligible trading fees back for three months. Your earnings go to your Cash balance, and your friend’s rebates go to theirs. Both are paid in CASH.
Referral codes can only be applied during signup in the Phantom mobile app. They can’t be applied during onboarding on the browser extension or in Phantom Terminal. Once you’ve joined through a referral on mobile, eligible swaps on the browser extension also count toward referral benefits.

How to get started
#
To receive referral benefits as a new user, sign up through a valid, active referral link or by scanning a referral QR code, or enter a referral code during signup.
Enter a referral code when creating your Phantom wallet
During signup, check that the referral code is included. If it isn’t added automatically, enter it manually on the referral screen.
Referral codes can’t be added after signup.
What referred users receive
#
Referred users can receive a percentage of the fees paid on their own eligible trades back as a rebate. This is credited to the Cash account rather than deducted from the fee upfront.
Eligible trades show your referral information in the fee breakdown, at transaction confirmation
The rebate percentage is shown in Phantom at transaction confirmation. Your three-month rebate period starts when the referral code is applied to your new account during mobile signup. Phantom doesn’t currently show your expiry date or remaining rebate period in the app.
When the rebate ends, the “10% discount on fees” message stops appearing during checkout. The rebate period may end earlier if the program or your eligibility ends.
Rates and earning windows may change.
What referrers earn and how payments work
#
You earn 25% of the net trading fees Phantom receives from each referred user's eligible trades. You begin earning once your friend funds their wallet and completes their first eligible trade.
For each referral, the three-month earning period starts when your referral code is applied to the user's new account during mobile signup. Only eligible trades within that period count toward your earnings. You won’t earn a fee share where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
Your referral earnings go to your Cash balance, and your referral's rebates go to theirs. Both are paid in CASH, at most once per day, after the recipient meets the $1 minimum payout threshold.
You may also need to complete identity verification, provide requested information, or maintain an eligible Cash account before receiving payment.
Eligible trades
#
Spot trades, perps, and predictions qualify as eligible trades. The trade must take place during the applicable rebate or earning window.

If you haven’t received a payout
#
Check the following:
The referred user signed up through a valid, active referral link, or entered the code at signup.
The trade took place during the applicable earning window and qualified for the program.
If you’re the referrer, the referred user funded their wallet and completed their first eligible trade.
You’ve met any applicable payout threshold and completed any requested verification.
Referrers won’t earn a fee share where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
