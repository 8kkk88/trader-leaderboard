Time & snapshot rules
1. Why wasn’t my order included in my Binance Alpha Points calculation for a specific date?
Binance Alpha Points are calculated based on a daily snapshot at 23:59:59 (UTC). All transactions are recorded based on this time zone.
For example, if you're in UTC+8 and completed a transaction on 23 April at 02:00 (UTC+8), it will be recorded as a transaction completed on 22 April at 18:00 (UTC), and counted toward 22 April.
Point expiry, spending & balance
1. Do Binance Alpha Points expire automatically?
Yes. Binance Alpha Points operate on a rolling 15-day cycle. Each Alpha Point will automatically expire exactly 15 days after the snapshot date.
2. When are points spent?
Binance Alpha Points are spent immediately when you confirm participation in an Alpha event (e.g., Alpha airdrops or TGEs).
The points you spend will be reflected in your total points on the following day.
3. Can I choose whether to spend points on an airdrop?
Yes. Participation in any event is optional. You can choose whether or not to spend your Binance Alpha Points.
4. What happens after an Alpha token gets listed for spot trading?
If an Alpha token gets listed for spot trading, transactions made after listing are not counted toward Alpha volume.
5. Does the wallet type matter for Alpha Volume tracking?
Yes. Only trades via Binance Wallet (Keyless) and Binance centralized exchange are eligible. If a wallet is imported or exported, its trading volume will not be counted toward Alpha volume.
Please note
: Re-imported wallets are no longer considered keyless.
6. How is my daily Binance Alpha Points balance calculated?
Your daily Binance Alpha Points balance is calculated using a rolling 15-day window. This means that your balance consists of all points earned and spent over the past 15 days. Here's how it works:
Example breakdown:
28 April - 12 May:
On 13 May, the 15-day window to review is between 28 April and 12 May.
Day 1 to Day 15: You earn 10 points per day from Day 1 to Day 10 and no points from Day 11 to Day 15, totaling 100 points.
Today (13 May): You spend 5 points, but your balance remains at 100 points on this day because the spending is reflected the next day.
29 April - 13 May:
On 14 May, the points earned on 28 April expire as the rolling window moves forward. The 15-day window is now between 29 April and 13 May.
Day 1 to Day 15 (29 April - 13 May): You have 90 points remaining.
Today (14 May): The 5 points spent on 13 May are now reflected, reducing your balance to 85 points.
30 April - 14 May:
On 15 May, the points earned on 29 April expire as the rolling window moves forward. The 15-day window is now between 30 April and 14 May.
Day 1 to Day 15 (30 April - 14 May): You have 75 points remaining.
Today (15 May): Your balance is now at 75 points.

1. What are the Binance Alpha Points?
Binance Alpha Points is a scoring system designed to evaluate user activity within the Binance Alpha and Binance Wallet ecosystem which determines user’s eligibility for campaigns, such as Token Generation Event (TGE) participation and Alpha token airdrops.
2. What are the Binance Alpha tokens?
Binance Alpha tokens refer to tokens that are featured under the Binance Alpha section under Binance Exchange and Binance Wallet. Binance Alpha was announced on 17-12-2024 as a platform designed to spotlight early-stage projects with potential to grow within the web3 ecosystem. Some tokens highlighted on Binance Alpha may be considered for future listings on Binance Exchange and Spot markets, while not guaranteed.

Binance Alpha Points are calculated daily as the sum of two components:
Balance Points:
These are based on your total eligible asset holdings across different accounts and wallets:
1. Binance Exchange:
Includes all spot-listed tokens and tokens held in your Binance Alpha account, aggregated across all your Binance Exchange accounts. RWUSD assets are included in the Binance Alpha Points calculation.
Futures unrealized PnL (profit or loss) at snapshot time is also included in the balance calculation.
2. Binance Wallet:
Includes the following tokens:
Binance Alpha tokens
Tokens listed on the Binance spot market
DeFi Assets:
DeFi BNB assets, including Staked Lista BNB (slisBNB) and slisBNB Non-Transferable Receipt (slisBNBx)
Booster Earn Receipt tokens, including: Treehouse ETH (tETH), Mitosis Matrix USDT (maUSDT), Mitosis BNB (miBNB), zkUSDT (zkUSDT), bwUSDT (bwUSDT), Staked USDu (sUSDu), bwU (bwU).
DeFi Earn Campaign receipt tokens, including: sUSDS, spUSDT, lisXAUT, sUSD1+, usd1-bank, fUSDT, steakUSDC, gtWETH, sUSDD, nWETH, nWBTC, sTRX, jUSDD, jJST, jSUN, nBASIS.

One side of the LP trading pair must be a Binance Alpha token, and the other side must be a token listed on the Binance spot market.
Examples of eligible LP pairs:

4. How are the Balance Points calculated?
Balance Points are calculated based on the following tiered structure:
100 USD to < 1,000 USD = 1 point/day
1,000 USD to < 10,000 USD = 2 points/day
10,000 USD to < 100,000 USD = 3 points/day
100,000 USD and above = 4 points/day
Snapshot timezone: UTC
For example, you have:
4,000 USD worth of eligible tokens in your Binance Exchange Spot Account.
3,000 USD worth of eligible tokens in your Binance Wallet.
5,000 USD worth of eligible PancakeSwap LP tokens held in the Binance Alpha Earn Hub.
Your total eligible assets for Balance Points = 4,000 USD + 3,000 USD + 5,000 USD = 12,000 USD
Since your total eligible assets are between 10,000 USD and 100,000 USD, you earn 3 Balance Points per day.
5. How are the Volume Points calculated?
Volume Points are calculated using the following formula:
2 USD = 1 point
4 USD = 2 points
8 USD = 3 points
16 USD = 4 points
32 USD = 5 points
Please note:
You’ll earn 1 additional point for each doubling of your purchase volume. For example, 64 USD = 6 points, 128 USD = 7 points, and so on.
6. Can I divide my 100,000 USD into multiple sub-accounts to earn more points on each account?
Only master accounts are eligible to earn Binance Alpha Points. Sub-account balances are merged into the master account’s score. Splitting balances across separate accounts to gain more points is not allowed.
7. What exchange rate is used to calculate my balance and/or trade value?
Alpha token values are measured using the USD-equivalent market price at the time of the trade. This ensures fair and consistent point tracking.
8. If I buy 600 USD worth of Alpha tokens and sell 500 USD later. Do I still get Volume Points for the 600 USD?
Yes. The token purchasing mechanic tracks your purchase volume, not your current holdings. So you’ll be credited for the full 600 USD worth of Alpha token you bought, even if you sell some later.
