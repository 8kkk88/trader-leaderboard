Frequently Asked Questions on Binance Wallet Service Fees
1. What are Binance Wallet service fees?
Binance Wallet offers its users an interface to engage in swaps through the Binance Swap. Binance Wallet charges a service fee for providing the tech interface.
Service fees are categorized into three groups based on the type of tokens involved in the transaction, and
differ depending whether the user accesses via the Binance Wallet App, Binance Wallet Web or Binance Agentic Wallet.
Group 1: Selected stablecoins, native tokens of major blockchains
, and Ondo & bStock tokenized securities.
Group 2: Binance Alpha tokens
Others: All tokens not included in Group 1 or Group 2

Please note:
No service fees are charged for swaps executed on the TON blockchain, via the Bridge function, or when using the “Use Exchange Balance” function.
Service fees may be amended from time to time at Binance Wallet’s sole discretion. Please follow our official channels to stay up to date.
2. How is the service fee calculated?
Service Fee = Transaction Amount * Fee Rate
Example (Binance Wallet App)
Swap 10,000 USD equivalent
between Group 1 and Group 2 tokens
:
10,000 USD * 0% = 0 USD
Swap 10,000 USD equivalent
between Group 1 and Others tokens
:
10,000 USD * 0.5% = 50 USD

3. When are service fees deducted?
Fees are applied when a swap is executed.
4. Will the fee rates or token lists change over time?
Fee rates and the token group list may be adjusted at Binance Wallet’s sole discretion as necessary to maintain fairness, compliance, and operational efficiency.
Please note:
Service fee is reflected in real time. Tokens in these groups are updated weekly and occasional discrepancies may occur. Please review the applicable service fees carefully when conducting your trades.
