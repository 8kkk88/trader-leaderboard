Dynamic Swap Fees Now Live: Enjoy Faster and More Efficient Multi-Chain Swaps
Announcement
At Bitget Wallet, we've always wanted trading crypto to be fast, simple, and stress-free. That's why we're excited to roll out
dynamic swap fees!
Now, your fees automatically adjust to real-time market conditions and liquidity, meaning swaps happen faster, smoother, and more efficiently—all without you lifting a finger. This upgrade makes onchain trading fairer, more flexible, and more cost-efficient for every user—whether you're swapping daily or moving large amounts across chains.
Multiple Swap Scenarios, Now with Significantly Lower Fees
To make everyday trading smoother and more affordable, we've optimized fees across several high-usage swap scenarios:
• Mainnet Coin ↔ Wrapped
Token
Swapping between native tokens and their wrapped versions now incurs 0 fees. This makes converting assets across onchain protocols easier and more affordable.
• Stablecoin ↔ Stablecoin
Stablecoin swaps happen constantly in crypto, so we've reduced fees in this high-frequency scenario. Now you can rebalance, move liquidity, or make fast swaps at a lower cost.
• Cross-Chain Swaps
Cross-chain swaps often involve complex routing and higher fees. Our optimized fee structure significantly reduces those costs, helping you transfer assets across chains faster and more efficiently.
These dynamic fees now apply across

gas-free transactions

Solana, Base, Morph, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Tron
.
With both upgrades combined, your transactions are now faster, cheaper, and far more user-friendly.
