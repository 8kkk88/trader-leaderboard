Swap crypto in Phantom
Swapping lets you trade one token for another across supported networks in Phantom. Phantom automatically performs a same-network swap or a
cross-chain swap
, depending on the tokens you choose.
What you need
#
A small amount of the network's native token to pay network fees. Some
Solana
and
EVM
swaps may be gasless.
For cross-chain swaps and swaps on Robinhood Chain, an amount of at least $2 (USD value).

Minimum swap amount
#
Cross-chain swaps and swaps on Robinhood Chain may have a minimum of $2 (USD value). Fixed costs like gas and bridge fees take up a large share of very small swaps. They can make a quote unavailable or leave you with much less than you sent. If your amount is below the minimum, increase it to continue.
Troubleshooting
#
Choose the section that matches what you see. Before trying another swap, check whether the original transaction was submitted.
Open
History
, select the swap, and review its status. If a transaction exists, use the blockchain explorer option to check it.
Pending: Wait for confirmation. For swaps between networks, see
Cross-chain swap stuck or taking a long time
.
Completed, but tokens are missing: Check the destination account and network. For Bitcoin, see
Bitcoin swap completed, but BTC is missing
.
Failed or no transaction was submitted: Follow the section matching your error. If the explorer still shows the transaction as pending, don’t treat it as a completed failure.
Quotes or swaps keep failing
#
Install any available Phantom update, then fully close and reopen the app. An outdated app can cause problems loading swap quotes.
If the error continues, note whether it affects one token or several. A problem with one token may involve its liquidity or trading restrictions. Errors across several tokens need further investigation.
Follow the section matching your error. If you’ve already tried its steps, contact Phantom Support.
“Insufficient funds” or “Not enough SOL/ETH/POL”
#
You may have enough of the token you want to swap but not enough of the network’s native token to pay the transaction fee.
Check the native-token balance on the network you’re using. For example, ETH on Ethereum and ETH on Base are separate balances; ETH on Ethereum cannot pay a Base network fee.
If you’re swapping the native token itself, reduce the amount so enough remains for the fee.
“No quotes available” or “Quote unavailable”
#
Phantom may be unable to find a supported route for the token pair or amount. This can happen when liquidity (the tokens available for trading) is too low, or when your swap is below the minimum amount.
If the swap hasn't been submitted, follow these steps:
For a cross-chain swap or a swap on Robinhood Chain, check that the amount is at least $2 (USD value). Very small swaps can fail because fixed network and bridge fees take up too much of the amount. If yours is below $2, increase the amount and request a new quote.
Wait a few minutes, then request a new quote.
Check whether the token has an active trading pool. See
Unable to swap a token due to low liquidity
.
For a cross-chain swap, an intermediate token such as USDC, ETH, or SOL may provide another route. Each additional swap can add fees and price impact, and a route is not guaranteed.
Swap simulation failed
#
Phantom checks a proposed transaction before submitting it. If that check fails, the swap cannot proceed as proposed.
Possible causes include insufficient liquidity, token trading restrictions, or slippage settings that don’t allow the swap to complete.
Try a smaller amount and check your slippage setting. Auto is suitable for most swaps. Increasing slippage tolerance allows a worse execution price, so review what you’re willing to accept before changing it. See
Adjust swap settings
.
A simulation failure alone does not establish that a token is malicious. If it continues, contact Phantom Support.
Swap failed after loading
#
Check
History
and the blockchain explorer before retrying. A transaction may still be pending even if the app displays an error.
If no transaction was submitted, or the explorer confirms it failed, try a smaller amount or request a fresh quote after a few minutes.
If the source transaction of a cross-chain swap completed, see
Cross-chain swap stuck or taking a long time
before starting another swap.
Price impact too high or received less than expected
#
Price impact is how much your own trade affects the price. Larger trades and lower-liquidity tokens usually have higher price impact.
Before confirming, check the amount you're quoted to receive and the price impact. A smaller trade or a token with more liquidity may reduce price impact.
If a completed swap returned significantly less than expected, review its transaction details and, for a cross-chain swap, both networks' explorer records.
Cross-chain swap stuck or taking a long time
#
Swaps between networks require blockchain confirmations and bridge processing. Bitcoin swaps can take longer because they also depend on Bitcoin network confirmations. The on-screen countdown is an estimate, not a guaranteed completion time.
Phantom uses several bridge providers, including LI.FI, Relay, deBridge, and Garden. The tracking tool depends on the provider handling your swap.
Open the swap in
History
and check the source and destination transactions using the available blockchain explorer links.
What you find
What to do
Source transaction is pending
Wait for confirmation. Don’t submit the swap again.
Source transaction completed; bridge is processing
Wait for bridge processing. Don’t submit the swap again.
Destination transaction completed; tokens are missing in Phantom
Check the destination account and network, then close and reopen Phantom. For Bitcoin, see
Bitcoin swap completed, but BTC is missing
.
Source transaction completed; bridge is stalled or failed
Contact Phantom Support.
If you cannot determine the swap’s status, contact Support before retrying. Phantom Support cannot reverse a bridge transaction or force completion, but can investigate and escalate to the relevant provider.
Resetting your wallet will not recover a missing destination transaction.
Bitcoin swap completed, but BTC is missing
#
Phantom supports separate two types of Bitcoin addresses: Native SegWit and Taproot. After a swap, your BTC can land in your Taproot address even when that address is not turned on in the app.
To troubleshoot, follow these steps:
Go to
Settings > Preferences > Preferred Bitcoin Address
.
Enable
Taproot
.
Close and reopen Phantom.
Check the balance in both Native SegWit and Taproot addresses.
A token ended up in your Cash account
#
An unsupported Solana token sent to your Cash address may not appear in your Cash balance.
For recovery steps, requirements, and help when the recovery option is missing, see
Recover crypto sent to your Cash account
.
Token cannot be sold or swapped
#
A token may have no usable liquidity or may restrict selling or transfers. A displayed price does not guarantee that you can sell it for that value.
Low liquidity: See
Unable to swap a token due to low liquidity
.
Frozen token: A frozen Solana token cannot be sent, sold, or swapped. Phantom cannot unfreeze it. See
What are frozen tokens on Solana?
Malicious or unfamiliar token: Avoid links or claim instructions associated with it. See
Report a token or collectible as spam
.
Changing swap settings cannot remove a token’s contract restrictions.
Watch-only address
#
If Phantom opens but you can’t use your wallet, your sign-in session may have expired after the app transferred from a backup.
Before resetting Phantom, make sure you have the original credentials for the wallet. You’ll need them to restore access. If you previously imported additional recovery phrases or private keys, have those in hand, too.
Go to
Settings > Security & Privacy > Reset App
. If you can’t access this option, uninstall Phantom and download it again.
Open Phantom and restore your wallets using the same Apple or Google account, recovery phrases, or private keys.
Resetting Phantom clears its local data. It doesn’t erase your wallets or funds.
“This account might be malicious”
#
Stop depositing funds into the account. This warning may indicate malicious account ownership or a wallet controlled by someone else.
Recovery options depend on what happened. Follow
the malicious-account guidance
to check the account and secure your wallet.
