# Jupiter Rewards Hub Overview
> The Jupiter Rewards Hub lists reward campaigns across Jupiter: Trading Card Game seasons, Active Staking Rewards, Gacha drops and Predict challenges.
The Jupiter Rewards Hub lists the reward campaigns run across Jupiter: Trading Card Game seasons and other trading campaigns, Active Staking Rewards for JUP stakers, Gacha pack drops, and Predict challenges. Each campaign card shows its status (Live or Ended), its reward pool, and **View details**; the **All**, **Live** and **Ended** filters sort them, and the page shows the total disbursed and, with a wallet connected, what you can claim.
Each campaign runs for a fixed period, with its own rules, eligible activity, reward currency, and distribution method. Campaigns are independent from one another: eligibility, points, and rewards do not carry over between campaigns.
## What is a campaign?
A campaign is a time-limited program where users earn rewards by using a Jupiter product (trading, opening Gacha packs, predicting) or, for ASR, by staking and voting with JUP. Each campaign defines:
* Which platforms and products are eligible
* Which activity earns rewards (token pairs, packs, markets), and at what rate
* How rewards are earned (trading points, cards, lootboxes)
* The reward currency and total pool
* How rewards are delivered: a claim window after the campaign ends, or an automatic airdrop for some campaigns
