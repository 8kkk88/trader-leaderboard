What are Uniswap Labs' fees?
Uniswap Interface Fees:
As of December 27, 2025 Uniswap Labs does not charge interface fees. This is separate from the Uniswap Protocol fee, which is voted on by Uniswap governance. Uniswap Labs is committed to simple and transparent pricing, and any fees charged by Uniswap Labs will remain updated here.
