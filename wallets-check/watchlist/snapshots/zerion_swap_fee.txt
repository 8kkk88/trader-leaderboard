At Zerion, we’re committed to full transparency, that means no hidden fees.
Zerion does not charge any fees for sending or receiving crypto from your wallet. The only fees we apply are when you use Zerion’s built-in Swap or Bridge features.
Below is a breakdown of the three types of fees you may encounter while using Zerion:
Network Fees (Gas Fees)
Every blockchain transaction requires a network fee (commonly known as
gas
) to be processed and validated by the network’s validators. These fees:
Are
not charged by Zerion
, but by the blockchain you’re transacting on
Vary depending on the network and current traffic (congestion)
Can be customized in Zerion: choose between Fast, Medium, or Custom fee levels right in the app
Zerion Fee (Swaps & Bridges Only)
When you use Zerion’s built-in
Swap
or
Bridge
features, a
0.67% service fee
is applied.
Here's what you should know:
Zerion Gold DNA holders are completely exempt from this fee, forever
Zerion Premium
users get a discount, bringing their fee down to
0.25%
All collected fees are stored in a secure, multisig "treasury-like" wallet, meaning multiple parties must approve any transaction from it. The funds are held safely until a community governance model is established.

Exchange Fees (External DEX or AMM)
When you place a trade that is routed through an external decentralized exchange (DEX) or automated market maker (AMM), their standard trading fee will automatically be included in your transaction total.
Zerion doesn’t control or collect these fees
These vary depending on the protocol used
